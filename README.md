## 🖤 October 5, 2026

Today is a day I will never forget.

Today, I lost my father.

There are some moments in life that words cannot
fully explain. This is one of them.

I may be far away, but I will always carry
the memories of my father with me.

Dad, thank you for everything you gave me,
for the life you gave me, and for the memories
that I will keep for the rest of my life.

You are no longer here with us,
but you will always have a place in my heart.

---

## 🕯️ For My Father

> You may be gone from my sight,

I will remember you.

I will remember your face.

I will remember the moments we shared.

And I will remember this day.

**October 5, 2026.**

## 🌿 Why I Created This Repository

This is not a software project.

This repository is a small digital place
where I can keep the memory of my father.

As time passes, I may add more photographs,
stories, memories, and words here.

I want this repository to remind me that
although someone can leave this world,
the memories they gave us can remain forever.
